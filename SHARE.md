# What you can hand to someone else

Hand them the skills and the empty notes. Do not hand them your filled `notes/`.

Safe to copy:

- `skills/`
- `SETUP.md`, `FIRST_START.md`, `README.md`, `PRIVACY.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `SHARE.md`
- Empty files under `notes/` after you have deleted names, quotes, dates, and evidence

Not safe to copy:

- `personal/` — paste-in calendar and any other local overlay. Never include this folder in a share
- `notes/people/`, `notes/promotions/`, `notes/performance/`, `notes/sources/`, `notes/meetings/`, `notes/dates.md` once they contain names
- A zip of your live vault

Skills must not contain your headcount, who is on a promotion track, who is a concern, partner names, or org names. Those belong in notes, written when that person runs `FIRST_START.md`.

If a skill still names a person, a count, or a vendor, that is a bug. Move the fact into `notes/` and generalize the skill.
