# Contributing

## Before committing

- Remove names, email addresses, account IDs, ticket IDs, customer details, and internal URLs.
- Replace secrets, tokens, credentials, and private hostnames with placeholders.
- Remove raw logs and screenshots unless they have been fully reviewed.
- Keep examples generic and reproducible.
- Check the diff for accidental sensitive content.

## Note style

Prefer concise notes that record:

- Context
- What changed or was investigated
- Evidence and constraints
- Decision or outcome
- Follow-up work
