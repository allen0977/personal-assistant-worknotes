# Stakeholders

Follow `pa-context`. Stakeholders live in `notes/people/stakeholders/<slug>.md`.

1. Ask the relationship before creating a file. A calendar role is not a name.
2. Ask what they control and what they currently need. Do not invent influence.
3. Follow the write protocol in `pa-context`. Copy `assets/stakeholder.md`.
