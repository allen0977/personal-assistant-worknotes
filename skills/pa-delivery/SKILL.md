# Delivery

Follow `pa-context`. `pa-scrum` is an alias. Do not assume Scrum.

Kinds that roll up here: `standup`, `review`, `retro`, `planning`, `refinement`, `checkin`.

1. Read `notes/delivery/INDEX.md` and `notes/calendar.md`, or `personal/calendar.md` if it exists.
2. A cycle page is `notes/delivery/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Do not overwrite. Append an addendum.
3. Record the goal, what was shown to users, what is still not done, and impediments. Scope cuts are decisions, not misses.
4. A retro is themes, not a person verdict. Do not copy a retro line into a performance file.
5. Do not invent that today's instance already happened.
6. Update the delivery index after a write.
